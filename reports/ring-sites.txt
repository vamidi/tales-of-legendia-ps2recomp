Ring-cursor constant scan -- tools/ring_sites.py --imm 4078,4079,4080

Section 7 of tales-blockcodec-doc gives 4078/4079 as the shortcut.  From
2004 the constant stopped being stable -- Symphonia's PlayStation 2 port
clears the ring with 4080 and Tales of Rebirth with 4079 -- so all three
are asked for at once here and the immediate is printed for every hit.

No 4078 and no 4079 anywhere on the I/O processor.  The two 4080
hits are SPU register values: KORGIVAG.IRX pairs 4080 with 4092 and
masks both with 0xFFCF, and LIBSD.IRX stores 4080 and 4092 as
halfwords into the SPU register block at 0xBF90xxxx.  Neither is a
length and neither is near a ring.  The decoder is on the Emotion
Engine only -- as in Symphonia 2004, and unlike Rebirth 2004 and
Destiny 2 2002, which both put a second copy on the IOP.
